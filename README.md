Turkey Run — Great Escape: a turkey auto-runs to the right through the farmyard, the barn and the kitchen, chased by the farmer with his pitchfork, jumping fences, hay bales and ovens, ducking and dodging the falling carving knives, grabbing corn, pumpkins and golden feathers — and escapes to the Safe Woods at 2000 metres before becoming Thanksgiving dinner. Title screen, HUD (score, distance, lives, farmer gap), caught and escaped end screens, restart, and WebAudio synth sounds. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make the best turkey escape game" bounty, $250 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Steer with ← → or A D, jump with Space, ↑ or click (hold for a higher jump), duck with ↓ or S, pause with P, mute with M.
